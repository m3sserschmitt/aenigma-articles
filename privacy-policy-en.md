# Privacy Policy

Aenigma does not collect, store, or process any personal information about its users.
The application does not request any personal data from the user in order to function.
User communications are end-to-end encrypted and, at the user’s choice, may be routed through the TOR (The Onion Router) network for complete anonymity.
All application data, including but not limited to the user’s cryptographic keys, remain stored exclusively on the device where the application runs.
