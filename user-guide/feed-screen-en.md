## The News Feed Screen

On this screen the user can view files and articles shared by other
users. Only files and articles shared within a certain period of time
will be visible, e.g., the last 3 months. The length of this period may vary from one version
to another. Given the distributed nature of the system, the user's contacts will
only have access to the files and articles shared by them *after* they connect via
QR code or link with them. Since posts are not stored in any centralized location,
users cannot access a post history similar to what
other social networking apps offer. There is no notion of a ***profile***.

**Note**: *If two users connected, for example, two months ago, they
will not be able to see each other's posts from two years ago. Only what has been shared in
the last two months will be visible.*

Users can create posts which are then shared exclusively with their
contact list.
